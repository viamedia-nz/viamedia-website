Commentary

Overall reach and delivery

The boosted post ran 21 August – 20 September on a $100 budget and spent $99.92. It reached 13,142 people in the NZ Performance Car audience, with 31,338 impressions at a frequency of 2.38.

Traffic

Meta recorded 2,152 link clicks on the ad, at $0.05 per click. CTR (all) was 14.27%. For context, Via Media's traffic campaigns on its own audiences typically record a cost per click of $0.25–$0.80.

Engagement

Meta recorded 2,613 post engagements. These include the link clicks above, 81 reactions, 2 comments and 3 shares.

Cost efficiency

CPM was $3.19, inside the $1–$4 range Via Media observes on its own NZ Performance Car and NZV8 audiences.

The website feature

Google Analytics recorded 2,129 views of the feature from 1,859 users between publication and 23 September. Over 1 August – 23 September it was the second most viewed page on nzperformancecar.co.nz, after the home page.

Most visitors came through the boosted post: Google Analytics recorded 1,638 users from the paid campaign. The feature also drew readers from Facebook shares (124), direct visits (48), Google and Bing search (33), and the NZ Performance Car newsletter (13).

Readers from search and the newsletter spent longest on the page, at around a minute and a half on average. Visitors from the paid boost averaged 3 seconds.

Search and AI visibility

Since publication the feature has appeared 480 times in Google search results, at an average position of 7.4, mostly for searches on the Fenix name — "fenix cooling", "fenix intercoolers" and "fenix radiators". Google also recorded 50 impressions of the feature inside its generative AI search features.

Measurement note

All campaign figures in this report come from Meta Ads Manager. Click and engagement figures are counted by Meta and are not verified server-side. They will not always match Google Analytics or a site's own analytics, because the two systems count differently. Treat them as Meta's indication of activity rather than a confirmed total. Website figures come from Google Analytics and Google Search Console for nzperformancecar.co.nz, and are counted separately from Meta's.

Reach is the number of unique accounts that saw your ads; summary reach deduplicates overlapping ad sets, unlike individual ad set reach. Impressions are the number of times your ads were on-screen, including multiple views by the same accounts. Frequency is the average number of times each account saw the campaign. Post engagements counts all interactions with the ad, including reactions, comments, shares, saves and video plays.
3-sec video plays is the number of times the video was played for at least three seconds. CTR (all) is the percentage of impressions where a click occurred out of the total number of impressions. Link clicks counts taps on the ad's link. It records the tap, not arrival at the destination. CPM (cost per 1,000 impressions) is the average cost of 1,000 impressions. CPC is the cost per link click.
