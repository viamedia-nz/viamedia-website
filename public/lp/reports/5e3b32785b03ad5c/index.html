Commentary

Overall reach and delivery

The campaign ran from 3 September to 30 September 2026 to the Auto Channel first-party trade audience, with a traffic objective. The full $300.00 budget was spent.

It reached 23,776 people and was shown 47,686 times, so each person saw it about 2.01 times across the month.

Traffic

Meta recorded 646 clicks on the ad's link to the Find out more page on blackfern.coop.

Meta also recorded 485 landing page views — its count of how many of those taps went on to load the page.

CTR (all) was 1.99, inside the 1 to 2.5 range we normally see on our first-party audiences.

Engagement

Meta recorded 708 post engagements. These include the link clicks above, 53 reactions, 7 saves and 1 comment.

Cost efficiency

CPC was $0.46, the cost of each link click. That is below the New Zealand all-industry median of about $0.87.

CPM was $6.29, the cost of showing the ad a thousand times. This is a tightly defined trade audience, so it costs more to reach than a broad consumer one.

Against August

August ran on the same budget, audience and delivery settings, with a different creative — the August reward-points draw rather than this month's membership message.

CPM rose from $5.02 to $6.29, so the same budget bought fewer impressions: 47,686 against 59,663. Reach was 23,776 against 30,259, and link clicks 646 against 864. CPC moved from $0.35 to $0.46.

CTR (all) rose from 1.65 to 1.99, and frequency held steady at about two views per person.

Measurement note

All figures in this report come from Meta Ads Manager. Click and engagement figures, including landing page views, are counted by Meta and are not verified server-side. They will not always match Google Analytics or a site's own analytics, because the two systems count differently. Treat them as Meta's indication of activity rather than a confirmed total.

Reach is the number of unique accounts that saw your ads; summary reach deduplicates overlapping ad sets, unlike individual ad set reach. Impressions are the number of times your ads were on-screen, including multiple views by the same accounts. Frequency is the average number of times each account saw the campaign. Post engagements counts all interactions with the ad, including reactions, comments, shares, saves and video plays.
3-sec video plays is the number of times the video was played for at least three seconds. CTR (all) is the percentage of impressions where a click occurred out of the total number of impressions. Link clicks counts taps on the ad's link. It records the tap, not arrival at the destination. CPM (cost per 1,000 impressions) is the average cost of 1,000 impressions. CPC is the cost per link click.
