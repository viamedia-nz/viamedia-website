Commentary

Overall reach and delivery

The campaign ran from 3 September to 4 October 2026 with a traffic objective. It served from the Auto Channel page to the Auto Channel first-party trade audience, and to people who have watched Automaster's videos or visited automaster.co.nz. The full budget was spent: $299.85 of $300.00.

It reached 17,001 people and was shown 55,401 times, so each person saw it about 3.26 times across the flight.

Ad set comparison

Meta divides the budget between the two ad sets and puts more behind the one that is delivering better. The carousel took $255.35 of the spend. It reached 15,736 people and Meta recorded 750 link clicks on it, at $0.34 each.

The video took $44.50. It reached 2,237 people and Meta recorded 104 link clicks, at $0.43 each. Its CTR (all) was higher, at 2.85.

Traffic

Meta recorded 854 clicks on the links to automaster.co.nz.

Automaster's site carries a Meta pixel that our ad account can use, so Meta also counts landing page views. It recorded 619 — its count of how many of those taps went on to load the page.

CTR (all) was 1.63, inside the 1 to 2.5 range we normally see on our first-party audiences.

Engagement

Meta recorded 3,242 post engagements. These include 2,361 video plays of at least three seconds: 1,729 on the video and 632 on the carousel.

Cost efficiency

CPC was $0.35, the cost of each link click. That is below the New Zealand all-industry median of about $0.87.

CPM was $5.41, the cost of showing the ads a thousand times. This is a tightly defined trade audience, so it costs more to reach than a broad consumer one.

Against July

July ran on the same $300 budget, audience and delivery settings.

CPM rose slightly, from $5.21 to $5.41, so the same budget bought fewer impressions: 55,401 against 57,561. Reach was 17,001 against 19,685, and frequency rose from 2.92 to 3.26.

Link clicks held close, at 854 against 869, and CPC was $0.35 in both months. CTR (all) moved from 1.74 to 1.63.

Measurement note

All figures in this report come from Meta Ads Manager. Click and engagement figures, including landing page views, are counted by Meta and are not verified server-side. They will not always match Google Analytics or a site's own analytics, because the two systems count differently. Treat them as Meta's indication of activity rather than a confirmed total.

Reach is the number of unique accounts that saw your ads; summary reach deduplicates overlapping ad sets, unlike individual ad set reach. Impressions are the number of times your ads were on-screen, including multiple views by the same accounts. Frequency is the average number of times each account saw the campaign. Post engagements counts all interactions with the ad, including reactions, comments, shares, saves and video plays.
3-sec video plays is the number of times the video was played for at least three seconds. CTR (all) is the percentage of impressions where a click occurred out of the total number of impressions. Link clicks counts taps on the ad's link. It records the tap, not arrival at the destination. CPM (cost per 1,000 impressions) is the average cost of 1,000 impressions. CPC is the cost per link click.
