Commentary

Overall reach and delivery

Two awareness campaigns promoted Chrome Showcase from 18 August to 4 October 2026, finishing on the second day of the event. One served from the NZ Performance Car page and one from the NZV8 page, each on a $250.00 budget. Both budgets were spent: $499.98 of $500.00.

The NZ Performance Car campaign reached 118,426 people and the NZV8 campaign reached 118,242. Together the ads were shown 656,619 times. Each person saw them about 2.81 times on NZ Performance Car and 2.74 times on NZV8 across the seven weeks.

Added together, the two campaigns reached 236,668. Some people follow both brands and will have seen both campaigns, so the number of individual people reached is lower than that.

Ad set comparison

Each campaign ran the same video to two audiences: the brand's first-party audience and lookalikes, and people who watched last year's Chrome Showcase video ads for 15 seconds or more. Meta divides each $250 budget between the two.

On NZ Performance Car the first-party ad set spent $127.72 and reached 83,287 people, and the 2025 viewers ad set spent $122.28 and reached 76,131. On NZV8 the first-party ad set spent $137.36 and reached 77,986, and the 2025 viewers ad set spent $112.62 and reached 77,968.

The two audiences overlap. 40,992 people saw both NZ Performance Car ad sets and 37,712 saw both NZV8 ad sets.

Engagement

Meta recorded 87,187 plays of the video lasting three seconds or more: 43,995 on NZ Performance Car and 43,192 on NZV8. That is about 13 plays for every 100 impressions.

Post engagements total 87,653. Alongside the video plays, Meta recorded 121 reactions, 30 shares, 4 saves and 311 clicks on the link to the Chrome Showcase links page.

Cost efficiency

CPM, the cost of showing the ads a thousand times, was $0.75 on NZ Performance Car and $0.77 on NZV8. That is below the $1 to $4 range we usually see on campaigns to our own first-party audiences.

Measurement note

All figures in this report come from Meta Ads Manager. Click and engagement figures are counted by Meta and are not verified server-side. They will not always match Google Analytics or a site's own analytics, because the two systems count differently. Treat them as Meta's indication of activity rather than a confirmed total.

Reach is the number of unique accounts that saw your ads; summary reach deduplicates overlapping ad sets, unlike individual ad set reach. Impressions are the number of times your ads were on-screen, including multiple views by the same accounts. Frequency is the average number of times each account saw the campaign. Post engagements counts all interactions with the ad, including reactions, comments, shares, saves and video plays.
3-sec video plays is the number of times the video was played for at least three seconds. CTR (all) is the percentage of impressions where a click occurred out of the total number of impressions. Link clicks counts taps on the ad's link. It records the tap, not arrival at the destination. CPM (cost per 1,000 impressions) is the average cost of 1,000 impressions. CPC is the cost per link click.
