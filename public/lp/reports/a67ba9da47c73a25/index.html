Commentary

Overall reach and delivery

The campaign ran from 5 October to 3 November on Auto Channel's trade audience: the Auto Channel industry newsletter list, customer lists from Auto Channel's workshop-sector partners, and lookalike audiences built from them. It reached 3,875 accounts and the ads were seen 25,272 times, an average of 6.52 times each. The campaign spent $399.84 of the $400.00 budget.

The audience is a defined trade group rather than the general public, so delivery stayed with the same people across the month.

Registrations and leads

The ad set was set to find the people most likely to complete a registration on hoist.nz, measured by Hoist's own Meta pixel, which Hoist has shared with Via Media's ad account.

Meta recorded 170 link clicks on the ads and 126 landing page views on hoist.nz. The pixel counted 5 registrations and 4 leads.

Video and carousel

The two ads shared one budget, and Meta gave the video more of the delivery: $227.70 against $172.14. The video drew most of the engagement, with 1,946 three-second plays. It recorded four of the five registrations and two of the four leads.

The carousel drew a higher click rate: CTR (all) of 1.50 against 1.20, and 91 link clicks at $1.89 each, against 79 at $2.88 for the video. It recorded one registration and two leads.

Added value: Auto Channel editorial

Separate to this campaign, as part of promoting the October issue of Auto Channel, Via Media boosted the issue's interview with Hoist's founders, Why workshops are switching to Hoist software, on the Auto Channel Facebook page.

This was an awareness push for the article rather than lead generation. It ran from 13 October to 1 November to the Auto Channel trade audience and recent Auto Channel site visitors, and reached 4,631 accounts. Meta recorded 126 link clicks on the post through to the article.

Across the campaign and the editorial boost together, Hoist reached 7,410 accounts, Meta's deduplicated figure for both. That is 3,535 more than the campaign reached on its own.

Measurement note

All figures in this report come from Meta Ads Manager. Click and engagement figures are counted by Meta and are not verified server-side. Registrations, leads and landing page views are counted by Hoist's own Meta pixel. They will not always match Google Analytics or Hoist's own records, because the systems count differently. Treat them as Meta's indication of activity rather than a confirmed total.

Reach is the number of unique accounts that saw your ads; summary reach deduplicates overlapping ad sets, unlike individual ad set reach. Impressions are the number of times your ads were on-screen, including multiple views by the same accounts. Frequency is the average number of times each account saw the campaign. Post engagements counts all interactions with the ad, including reactions, comments, shares, saves and video plays. Registrations are Complete Registration events counted by Hoist's Meta pixel. Leads are Lead events counted by Hoist's Meta pixel.
3-sec video plays is the number of times the video was played for at least three seconds. CTR (all) is the percentage of impressions where a click occurred out of the total number of impressions. Link clicks counts taps on the ad's link. It records the tap, not arrival at the destination. CPM (cost per 1,000 impressions) is the average cost of 1,000 impressions. CPC is the cost per link click.
