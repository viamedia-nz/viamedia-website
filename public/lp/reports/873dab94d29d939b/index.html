Commentary

Overall reach and delivery

The two Meta ads reached 113,785 accounts in the NZ Performance Car audience and were seen 386,874 times, an average of 3.40 times each across October. For an awareness campaign, that level of repetition helps the $399 offer stay front of mind. The campaigns spent $499.84 of the $500.00 budget, at a combined CPM of $1.29, inside the $1–$4 range Via Media sees on its own first-party audiences.

Static and reel

The static and the reel ran side by side for the full month, each on $250.00, to the same audience and with the same delivery settings. The static reached more people: 94,874 accounts at a frequency of 2.21 and a CPM of $1.19. The reel reached 52,554 accounts but showed to each of them more often, at a frequency of 3.37 and a CPM of $1.41. Of those, 33,643 people saw both ads.

Engagement

The reel recorded 22,044 three-second video plays, from 177,212 impressions. Almost all of the campaign's 22,212 post engagements came from the reel, which is what a video format is built to collect. The static carries the offer in a single frame, so its role is reach rather than interaction.

Website pop-up and banners

On nzperformancecar.co.nz, the pop-up recorded 9,949 impressions and 2,352 clicks during October, a CTR of 23.64. A pop-up sits in front of the page until the reader acts on it, so it draws a much higher click rate than a standard banner.

The banners ran from 5 to 31 October in three sizes and delivered 10,002 impressions against the 10,000 booked, with 5 clicks recorded by Google Ad Manager.

Measurement note

All Meta figures in this report come from Meta Ads Manager. Click and engagement figures are counted by Meta and are not verified server-side. They will not always match Google Analytics or a site's own analytics, because the two systems count differently. Treat them as Meta's indication of activity rather than a confirmed total. Website figures come from the NZ Performance Car website's pop-up reporting and from Google Ad Manager, and are counted separately from Meta's.

Reach is the number of unique accounts that saw your ads; summary reach deduplicates overlapping ad sets, unlike individual ad set reach. Impressions are the number of times your ads were on-screen, including multiple views by the same accounts. Frequency is the average number of times each account saw the campaign. Post engagements counts all interactions with the ad, including reactions, comments, shares, saves and video plays.
3-sec video plays is the number of times the video was played for at least three seconds. CTR (all) is the percentage of impressions where a click occurred out of the total number of impressions. Link clicks counts taps on the ad's link. It records the tap, not arrival at the destination. CPM (cost per 1,000 impressions) is the average cost of 1,000 impressions. CPC is the cost per link click.
