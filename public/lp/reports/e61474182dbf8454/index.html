Commentary

Overall reach and delivery

Three awareness campaigns ran from 4 September to 1 October 2026, each served from the Mount Shop page to a different audience: NZ Performance Car's first-party audience, the Auto Channel trade audience with Mount Shop's own customer lists, and a 4WD audience. The full budgets were spent: $1,299.88 of $1,300.00.

Together the ads were shown 947,451 times and reached up to 232,876 accounts. The three campaigns ran in separate ad accounts, so Meta cannot remove people who saw more than one of them. The true number of unique people sits between 90,818, the largest single campaign, and that total.

NZV8

The campaign reached 59,124 people in the NZ Performance Car audience and was shown 237,444 times, about 4.02 times each.

Meta divided the budget evenly between the two ad sets: $149.84 to the static and $150.16 to the Reel. The Reel recorded 13,080 video plays of at least three seconds and nearly all of the campaign's 13,216 post engagements.

Auto Channel trade

The campaign reached 90,818 people and was shown 348,511 times, about 3.84 times each.

Meta put most of the budget behind the static, $466.46, and $33.48 behind the Reel. The Reel recorded 344 video plays of at least three seconds.

4WD

The campaign reached 82,934 people and was shown 361,496 times, about 4.36 times each. Meta recorded 25,237 video plays of at least three seconds.

The interests ad set took $339.17 of the budget and recorded 12,782 of those plays. The retargeting ad set took $134.18 and recorded 9,827. The lookalike ad set took $26.59.

Cost efficiency

CPM, the cost of showing the ads a thousand times, was $1.26 on NZV8, $1.43 on Auto Channel and $1.38 on 4WD. All three sit within the $1 to $4 range we see on our own first-party audiences.

Against August

The Auto Channel and 4WD campaigns ran on the same budgets, audiences and delivery settings as in August.

Auto Channel: CPM rose from $1.30 to $1.43, so the same budget bought fewer impressions: 348,511 against 384,426. Reach was 90,818 against 95,240. In August Meta put $193.69 behind the Reel, which recorded 17,592 video plays; this month it put $33.48 behind it, so video plays were 344.

4WD: CPM held close, at $1.38 against $1.34. Reach was 82,934 against 106,937, and frequency rose from 3.48 to 4.36. Video plays rose from 22,218 to 25,237.

Measurement note

All figures in this report come from Meta Ads Manager. Click and engagement figures are counted by Meta and are not verified server-side. They will not always match Google Analytics or a site's own analytics, because the two systems count differently. Treat them as Meta's indication of activity rather than a confirmed total.

Reach is the number of unique accounts that saw your ads; summary reach deduplicates overlapping ad sets, unlike individual ad set reach. Impressions are the number of times your ads were on-screen, including multiple views by the same accounts. Frequency is the average number of times each account saw the campaign. Post engagements counts all interactions with the ad, including reactions, comments, shares, saves and video plays.
3-sec video plays is the number of times the video was played for at least three seconds. CTR (all) is the percentage of impressions where a click occurred out of the total number of impressions. Link clicks counts taps on the ad's link. It records the tap, not arrival at the destination. CPM (cost per 1,000 impressions) is the average cost of 1,000 impressions. CPC is the cost per link click.
