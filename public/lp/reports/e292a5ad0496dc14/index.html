Commentary

Overall reach and delivery

The boosted post ran 18 August – 27 September on a $100 budget and spent $99.91. It reached 11,394 people, with 24,379 impressions at a frequency of 2.14.

Traffic

Meta recorded 982 link clicks on the ad, at $0.10 per click. CTR (all) was 10.86%. For context, Via Media's traffic campaigns on its own audiences typically record a cost per click of $0.25–$0.80.

Engagement

Meta recorded 3,460 post engagements. These include the link clicks above, 143 reactions, 13 comments, 6 shares and 5 saves.

The website feature

Google Analytics recorded 1,302 views of the feature from 1,080 users between publication and 27 September. Over that period it was the third most viewed page on nzv8.co.nz, after the home page and one other feature.

Most visits came through the boosted post: Google Analytics recorded 983 visits from the paid campaign. The feature also drew readers from Facebook shares and referrals (131 visits), the NZV8 newsletter (16), direct visits (13), and Google search (11).

Readers from Google search spent longest on the page, at 1 minute 45 seconds on average. Visitors from the paid boost averaged 17 seconds.

Search and AI visibility

Since publication the feature has appeared 224 times in Google search results, at an average position of 5.7, mostly for searches on "thunderpark" and "thunderpark hastings". Google also recorded 39 impressions of the feature inside its generative AI search features.

Measurement note

All campaign figures in this report come from Meta Ads Manager. Click and engagement figures are counted by Meta and are not verified server-side. They will not always match Google Analytics or a site's own analytics, because the two systems count differently. Treat them as Meta's indication of activity rather than a confirmed total. Website figures come from Google Analytics and Google Search Console for nzv8.co.nz, and are counted separately from Meta's.

Reach is the number of unique accounts that saw your ads; summary reach deduplicates overlapping ad sets, unlike individual ad set reach. Impressions are the number of times your ads were on-screen, including multiple views by the same accounts. Frequency is the average number of times each account saw the campaign. Post engagements counts all interactions with the ad, including reactions, comments, shares, saves and video plays.
3-sec video plays is the number of times the video was played for at least three seconds. CTR (all) is the percentage of impressions where a click occurred out of the total number of impressions. Link clicks counts taps on the ad's link. It records the tap, not arrival at the destination. CPM (cost per 1,000 impressions) is the average cost of 1,000 impressions. CPC is the cost per link click.
