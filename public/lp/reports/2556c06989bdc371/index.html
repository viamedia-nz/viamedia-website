Commentary

Overall reach and delivery

Three awareness campaigns ran from 8 October to 1 November 2026, each served from the Mount Shop page to a different audience: NZ Performance Car's first-party audience, the Auto Channel trade audience with Mount Shop's own customer lists, and a 4WD audience. The full budgets were spent: $1,299.45 of $1,300.00.

Together the ads were shown 883,026 times and reached up to 230,490 accounts. The three campaigns ran in separate ad accounts, so Meta cannot remove people who saw more than one of them. The true number of unique people sits between 98,038, the largest single campaign, and that total.

NZV8

The campaign reached 49,390 people in the NZ Performance Car audience and was shown 219,220 times, about 4.44 times each.

Meta divided the budget close to evenly between the two ad sets: $143.93 to the static and $155.96 to the Reel. The Reel recorded 13,742 video plays of at least three seconds and nearly all of the campaign's 13,862 post engagements.

Auto Channel trade

The campaign reached 83,062 people and was shown 299,551 times, about 3.61 times each.

Meta put almost all of the budget behind the static, $489.66, and $10.09 behind the Reel. The Reel recorded 169 video plays of at least three seconds.

4WD

The campaign reached 98,038 people and was shown 364,255 times, about 3.72 times each. Meta recorded 30,759 video plays of at least three seconds.

The interests ad set took $291.83 of the budget and recorded 20,029 of those plays. The retargeting ad set took $113.81 and recorded 8,562. The lookalike ad set took $94.17 and recorded 2,168.

Cost efficiency

CPM, the cost of showing the ads a thousand times, was $1.37 on NZV8, $1.67 on Auto Channel and $1.37 on 4WD. All three sit within the $1 to $4 range we see on our own first-party audiences.

Against September

All three campaigns ran on the same budgets, audiences and delivery settings as in September. The October flights started on 8 October, against 4 September, so each budget was spent over a slightly shorter period.

NZV8: CPM rose from $1.26 to $1.37, so the same budget bought fewer impressions: 219,220 against 237,444. Reach was 49,390 against 59,124, and frequency rose from 4.02 to 4.44. Video plays rose from 13,080 to 13,742.

Auto Channel: CPM rose from $1.43 to $1.67, and impressions were 299,551 against 348,511. Reach was 83,062 against 90,818. Meta put $10.09 behind the Reel, against $33.48 in September, so video plays were 169 against 344.

4WD: CPM held level, at $1.37 against $1.38. Reach rose from 82,934 to 98,038 and frequency fell from 4.36 to 3.72, so the budget reached more people, fewer times each. Video plays rose from 25,237 to 30,759. The lookalike ad set took a larger share of the budget: $94.17 against $26.59.

Measurement note

All figures in this report come from Meta Ads Manager. Click and engagement figures are counted by Meta and are not verified server-side. They will not always match Google Analytics or a site's own analytics, because the two systems count differently. Treat them as Meta's indication of activity rather than a confirmed total.

Reach is the number of unique accounts that saw your ads; summary reach deduplicates overlapping ad sets, unlike individual ad set reach. Impressions are the number of times your ads were on-screen, including multiple views by the same accounts. Frequency is the average number of times each account saw the campaign. Post engagements counts all interactions with the ad, including reactions, comments, shares, saves and video plays.
3-sec video plays is the number of times the video was played for at least three seconds. CTR (all) is the percentage of impressions where a click occurred out of the total number of impressions. Link clicks counts taps on the ad's link. It records the tap, not arrival at the destination. CPM (cost per 1,000 impressions) is the average cost of 1,000 impressions. CPC is the cost per link click.
