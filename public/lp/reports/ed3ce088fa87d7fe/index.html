Delivery status

Both ad sets optimise to the Complete Registration event on HPA's pixel, which HPA has shared into Via Media's ad account. Each has already recorded well over the roughly 30–40 weekly conversions Meta looks for to exit the learning phase — 626 on NZPC and 439 on NZV8 in the first four days — so current figures reflect stable, optimised delivery rather than early calibration.

Measurement note

All figures come from Meta Ads Manager. Landing page views and confirmed entries are counted through the Complete Registration event on HPA's pixel. None of this is verified server-side against HPA's own competition database, and it will not always match HPA's internal count, because the two systems count differently. Treat these as Meta's indication of activity rather than a confirmed total.

Reach is the number of unique accounts that saw your ads; summary reach here is a cross-account sum, not a deduplicated figure.
Impressions are the number of times your ads were on-screen, including repeat views by the same accounts.
Frequency is the average number of times each account saw the campaign.
Link clicks counts taps on the ad's link — the tap, not arrival at the destination.
Landing page views are visits Meta's pixel recorded on HPA's entry page after a click.
Confirmed entries are Complete Registration events recorded by HPA's pixel when the competition entry form is submitted.
CPA (cost per acquisition) is amount spent divided by confirmed entries.
LPV → entry rate is the share of landing page views that went on to record a confirmed entry.
