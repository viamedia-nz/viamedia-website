Overall reach and delivery

Six campaigns ran across the two ad accounts between 15 September and 11 October: the two $2,000 lead campaigns, the Legends launch video boosts and the first build update boosts. Together they reached 362,390 accounts with 870,149 impressions for $4,423.88. Within each account, reach across all three campaigns was 221,601 on NZ Performance Car and 140,789 on NZV8.

Competition entries

Meta has recorded 9,926 confirmed entries through HPA's pixel: 6,019 from NZ Performance Car and 3,907 from NZV8. Combined cost per entry is $0.39, down from $0.55 at the first report. NZ Performance Car sits at $0.32 and NZV8 at $0.50.

The share of landing page views that went on to a confirmed entry rose from 48.57% to 69.27%. Cost per link click held steady on NZ Performance Car at $0.22 and moved from $0.29 to $0.32 on NZV8, so the lower cost per entry came from more visitors completing the form rather than from cheaper clicks. Since the first report, the campaigns have added 8,861 entries at $0.37 each.

Legends launch video

The launch video ran as a $200 traffic boost on each brand from 23 September to 4 October. Meta recorded 4,540 link clicks across the two, at $0.10 on NZ Performance Car and $0.08 on NZV8, with click-through rates of 5.57 and 6.80. HPA's pixel recorded 4,086 landing page views from these clicks, and the video had 22,719 three-second plays.

Build update boosts

The first build update post has been boosted on each brand since 6 October with an engagement objective, and runs to 20 October. To 11 October it has spent $151.12 of $400.00, reached 25,640 accounts on NZ Performance Car and 8,412 on NZV8, and recorded 21,315 post engagements, including 19,554 three-second video plays. The NZV8 post drew 1,443 reactions.

Measurement note. All figures in this report come from Meta Ads Manager. Landing page views and confirmed entries are counted through the Complete Registration event on HPA's pixel, which HPA has shared into Via Media's ad accounts. Click and engagement figures are counted by Meta and are not verified server-side. None of this is checked against HPA's own competition database, and it will not always match HPA's internal count or Google Analytics, because the systems count differently. Treat these as Meta's indication of activity rather than a confirmed total.
Reach is the number of unique accounts that saw your ads; account totals deduplicate overlapping campaigns, and summary reach here is a cross-account sum.
Impressions are the number of times your ads were on-screen, including multiple views by the same accounts.
Frequency is the average number of times each account saw the campaign.
Post engagements counts all interactions with the ad, including reactions, comments, shares, saves and video plays.
3-sec video plays is the number of times the video was played for at least three seconds.
CTR (all) is the percentage of impressions where a click occurred out of the total number of impressions.
Link clicks counts taps on the ad's link. It records the tap, not arrival at the destination.
CPM (cost per 1,000 impressions) is the average cost of 1,000 impressions. CPC is the cost per link click.
Landing page views are visits HPA's pixel recorded on the entry page after a click. Confirmed entries are Complete Registration events recorded when the entry form is submitted.
CPA is amount spent divided by confirmed entries. LPV → entry rate is the share of landing page views that went on to a confirmed entry.
