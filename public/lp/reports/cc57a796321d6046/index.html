Commentary

Overall reach and delivery

The boosted post ran from 14 October to 4 November on NZ Performance Car's own audience and lookalike audiences built from it. It reached 12,925 accounts, with 24,270 impressions at a frequency of 1.88. The campaign spent $99.86 of the $100.00 budget.

Traffic

Meta recorded 1,423 link clicks on the ad, at $0.07 per click. For context, Via Media's traffic campaigns on its own audiences typically record a cost per click of $0.25–$0.80.

Engagement

Meta recorded 1,537 post engagements. These include the link clicks above, 67 reactions, 6 comments, 15 shares and 9 saves.

Website sponsored post

Google Analytics recorded 1,614 views of the feature from 1,403 users between publication and 5 November. Over that period it was the second most viewed page on nzperformancecar.co.nz, after the home page.

Most visits came through the boosted post: Google Analytics recorded 1,278 visits from the paid campaign. The feature also drew readers from Facebook shares and referrals (80 visits), Google search (59), direct visits (33) and the NZ Performance Car newsletter (7).

Readers who arrived from Facebook shares and from Google search spent longest on the page, at 1 minute 56 seconds and 1 minute 18 seconds on average. Visitors from the paid boost averaged 21 seconds.

Search and AI visibility

Since publication the feature has appeared 3,083 times in Google search results, at an average position of 6.5, for searches such as "sim racing setup", "sim racing" and "sim racing cockpit". It also appeared for longer, question-style searches, such as one asking for the best components to build a realistic driving simulator at home.

Google recorded 930 impressions of the feature inside its generative AI search features over the same period.

Measurement note

All campaign figures in this report come from Meta Ads Manager. Click and engagement figures are counted by Meta and are not verified server-side. They will not always match Google Analytics or a site's own analytics, because the two systems count differently. Treat them as Meta's indication of activity rather than a confirmed total. Website figures come from Google Analytics and Google Search Console for nzperformancecar.co.nz, and are counted separately from Meta's.

Reach is the number of unique accounts that saw your ads; summary reach deduplicates overlapping ad sets, unlike individual ad set reach. Impressions are the number of times your ads were on-screen, including multiple views by the same accounts. Frequency is the average number of times each account saw the campaign. Post engagements counts all interactions with the ad, including reactions, comments, shares, saves and video plays.
3-sec video plays is the number of times the video was played for at least three seconds. CTR (all) is the percentage of impressions where a click occurred out of the total number of impressions. Link clicks counts taps on the ad's link. It records the tap, not arrival at the destination. CPM (cost per 1,000 impressions) is the average cost of 1,000 impressions. CPC is the cost per link click.
