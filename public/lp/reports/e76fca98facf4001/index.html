Commentary

Overall reach and delivery

Both campaigns have been live since 9 October and are on schedule. To 30 October they have spent $328.18 of the $600.00 budget, with 16 days still to run.

The Auto Channel campaign has reached 25,195 accounts in the automotive trade audience. The NZ Performance Car campaign has reached 41,418 accounts in the enthusiast audience. Together that is 66,613 accounts, with 210,575 impressions.

Frequency is 3.72 on Auto Channel and 2.82 on NZ Performance Car. The trade audience is the smaller of the two, so people in it see the ad more often for the same spend.

Both audiences include people who have watched Exedy's earlier video ads on Auto Channel and NZ Performance Car.

Where the ads were seen

Both campaigns are set to run across New Zealand. In the Auckland Region the ad has reached 9,178 trade accounts and 14,118 enthusiast accounts. That is about a third of the spend in each account: 32% on Auto Channel and 31% on NZ Performance Car. Waikato and Canterbury are the next largest regions.

Engagement

Meta recorded 137 post engagements across the two campaigns, and 129 clicks on the link to exedy.co.nz. Most of the engagement count is those link clicks. CTR (all) is 0.11 on Auto Channel and 0.08 on NZ Performance Car.

Cost efficiency

The campaigns are buying impressions at $1.75 per 1,000 on Auto Channel and $1.41 per 1,000 on NZ Performance Car. Both sit within the $1–$4 range Via Media sees for campaigns to its own first-party audiences.

Measurement note

All figures in this report come from Meta Ads Manager. Click and engagement figures are counted by Meta and are not verified server-side. They will not always match Google Analytics or a site's own analytics, because the two systems count differently. Treat them as Meta's indication of activity rather than a confirmed total.

Reach is the number of unique accounts that saw your ads; summary reach deduplicates overlapping ad sets, unlike individual ad set reach. Impressions are the number of times your ads were on-screen, including multiple views by the same accounts. Frequency is the average number of times each account saw the campaign. Post engagements counts all interactions with the ad, including reactions, comments, shares, saves and video plays.
3-sec video plays is the number of times the video was played for at least three seconds. CTR (all) is the percentage of impressions where a click occurred out of the total number of impressions. Link clicks counts taps on the ad's link. It records the tap, not arrival at the destination. CPM (cost per 1,000 impressions) is the average cost of 1,000 impressions. CPC is the cost per link click.
