Overall reach and delivery

Four paid campaigns ran across the two ad accounts between 6 October and 9 November: the two $500 lead campaigns and the first build update boosts. Together they reached 169,696 accounts with 275,314 impressions for $1,346.75. Within each account, reach was 111,637 on NZ Performance Car and 58,059 on NZV8.

Competition entries

Meta has recorded 3,008 confirmed entries through HPA's pixel since the November lead campaigns started on 14 October: 1,927 from NZ Performance Car and 1,081 from NZV8. Combined cost per entry is $0.31, with NZ Performance Car at $0.25 and NZV8 at $0.44. Across both flights since 15 September, the lead campaigns have recorded 13,367 entries at $0.37 each.

Against the October flight

The November flight ran on a quarter of October's budget, so rates are the fair comparison. Cost per entry fell from $0.39 to $0.31. Cost per link click was steady, moving from $0.22 to $0.21 on NZ Performance Car and holding at $0.32 on NZV8, while CPM rose on both brands. The lower cost per entry came from a higher share of visitors completing the form: 88.76% of landing page views went on to a confirmed entry, up from 70.15%.

Build update boosts

The first build update post ran as a $200 engagement boost on each brand from 6 to 20 October. Over the full flight it reached 52,893 accounts on NZ Performance Car and 16,313 on NZV8, and recorded 51,792 post engagements, including 48,173 three-second video plays. The NZV8 post drew 2,912 reactions.

Website pop-ups

The HPA Legends pop-up ran on both brand websites from 12 October to 1 November, linking to the entry page. It was shown 6,206 times and clicked 1,054 times, a click-through rate of 16.98%. NZ Performance Car recorded 740 clicks from 3,585 impressions and NZV8 314 from 2,621.

Build update posts

Episode 1 went out on both brands on 6 October and carried the build update boost, which is reported in the campaign table. Unpaid, it added 1,950 Facebook views on NZ Performance Car and 2,539 on NZV8, and the NZ Performance Car Instagram post reached 5,786 accounts. Episodes 2 and 3 followed on 20 October and 3 November as unpaid posts. Across all three episodes, the unpaid posts recorded 23,813 video views on Facebook and Instagram.

Measurement note. Paid campaign figures in this report come from Meta Ads Manager. Landing page views and confirmed entries are counted through the Complete Registration event on HPA's pixel, which HPA has shared into Via Media's ad accounts. Click and engagement figures are counted by Meta and are not verified server-side. Website pop-up figures are counted on the NZ Performance Car and NZV8 websites, and build update post figures come from Facebook and Instagram's own post insights; both are counted separately from Meta's ad reporting. None of this is checked against HPA's own competition database, and it will not always match HPA's internal count or Google Analytics, because the systems count differently. Treat these as an indication of activity rather than a confirmed total.

Creative

Reach is the number of unique accounts that saw your ads; account totals deduplicate overlapping campaigns, and summary reach here is a cross-account sum.
Impressions are the number of times your ads were on-screen, including multiple views by the same accounts.
Frequency is the average number of times each account saw the campaign.
Post engagements counts all interactions with the ad, including reactions, comments, shares, saves and video plays.
3-sec video plays is the number of times the video was played for at least three seconds.
CTR (all) is the percentage of impressions where a click occurred out of the total number of impressions.
Link clicks counts taps on the ad's link. It records the tap, not arrival at the destination.
CPM (cost per 1,000 impressions) is the average cost of 1,000 impressions. CPC is the cost per link click.
Landing page views are visits HPA's pixel recorded on the entry page after a click. Confirmed entries are Complete Registration events recorded when the entry form is submitted.
CPA is amount spent divided by confirmed entries. LPV → entry rate is the share of landing page views that went on to a confirmed entry.
Pop-up impressions are the times the pop-up was shown on the website. Pop-up clicks are clicks on it.
Post video views are the unpaid video views Facebook and Instagram count on the posts themselves. Instagram reach is the number of unique accounts that saw the post.
