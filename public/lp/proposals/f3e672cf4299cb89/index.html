What we found

Strong content, ready for more reach

We reviewed your Pages, websites, and tracking before we wrote this proposal.

Working well

Your content is strong

You have posted about six times a week since August. Your locally shot posts get the most response by a wide margin, in particular Beat the B-Series and your customer handovers.

Opportunity

Most posts reach few people

Organic posts on a new Page reach a small share of your audience. The posts that earn attention need paid distribution behind them.

Fix needed

Your ad account is blocked

Meta sets an ad account's currency when the account is created, and it cannot be changed after that. A new NZD account removes the currency fault.

Gap

Your tracking records visits only

On the pages we checked, the Meta pixel on denzaeastauckland.nz records page views only. We found no Meta pixel on denzachristchurch.nz. Both sites have Google Tag Manager, so this is quick to fix.

To confirm

Christchurch shares the Auckland Page

Your permanent Christchurch showroom opened on Manchester Street in late September. We could not find a Facebook Page for DENZA Christchurch. If there is not one, Christchurch buyers currently see DENZA East Auckland content only.

The plan

Set up properly, then grow both showrooms

Your timeline builds trust. Your ads build reach.

1

Setup

  • Audit who has access to your Pages, Business Manager, and tracking, and confirm that Harmony owns each one.
  • A dedicated NZD Meta ad account, which removes the currency fault.
  • Meta pixel events on both websites through Google Tag Manager: test-drive bookings, enquiries, and finance enquiries.
  • Google Ads conversion tracking and GA4 key events on both sites.
  • Your customer list uploaded, to exclude existing owners and to guide targeting.
  • A DENZA Christchurch Facebook and Instagram presence, subject to DENZA brand guidelines.
  • Tracking tags on your Instagram link-in-bio links, so organic traffic shows in your reports.
  • Your Dealer Studio inventory feed connected for vehicle ads, subject to platform availability in New Zealand.
2
$2,000 a month each — Auckland and Christchurch

Meta advertising

  • Separate campaigns for each showroom, so each budget is spent where you intend.
  • Model-led ads for the right buyers: B5 and B8 for 4WD, towing, and premium SUV buyers; D9 for families.
  • Your OEM model content runs as targeted ads. These ads do not appear on your Page timeline, so the timeline stays focused on your people, events, and customers.
  • Your best local posts get paid support, so more of the right people see them.
  • Retargeting for people who viewed your model pages or watched your videos.
3
$2,000 a month each — Auckland and Christchurch

Google Ads

  • Search campaigns for each showroom, aimed at people who search for DENZA and its models in your areas.
  • Category searches from buyers who compare premium 4WD and family SUVs.
  • Your Google review ratings in your ads: 4.8 from 67 reviews in East Auckland and 5.0 from 13 in Christchurch.
  • Brand and non-brand searches reported separately, so you can see what paid search adds.
4

Social media management

  • Your Pages stay as active as they are now. What changes is the mix.
  • Timeline posts focus on what performs best: customers, events, handovers, and your team. We write, schedule, and publish up to eight a month per Page on Facebook and Instagram, alongside the local posts your team already creates.
  • OEM model content moves into ads, where it reaches far more of the right people than it does as a timeline post. We produce up to six new ad variants a month for each showroom.
  • Comment monitoring stays with your team, as you requested.
5

Lead generation

  • The booked test drive is the main measure of success. We also track enquiries, calls, and messages.
  • All ads send people to your own websites and Pages, not to the national DENZA site.
  • Events as a lead source: ads that fill events like Beat the B-Series and capture registrations as leads.
  • Later phase: sale outcomes from your lead system fed back to Meta and Google, so the platforms aim for buyers and not only form fills. This depends on your lead system.
6

Monthly reporting

  • One report each month for both showrooms and all platforms: spend, reach, leads, cost per lead, and test drives.
  • Clear insights and the actions we plan for the next month.
  • A monthly review call.
