What we found

How Kruzin Kustoms shows up online today

We looked at your website, search results, AI answers, social channels, and advertising as a potential customer would see them, as the first step towards a full audit of your online presence and marketing channels.

Working well

A name the scene already knows

You won Supreme Business of the Year at the 2026 Yorb Manawatū Business Awards, along with the Marketing, Trades & Services, and Medium Business awards. Your builds have been NZV8 cover cars and featured in the US magazine Classic Truck Performance. Your Facebook Page has 12,600 followers, and each finished build draws a strong response.

Gap

Missing from search and AI answers for your trade

For “hot rod shop NZ”, “hot rod builder NZ”, “restomod builder NZ”, “best custom car builder in New Zealand”, and “hot rod parts NZ”, Kruzin Kustoms appeared only in paid ads. When we asked Perplexity and Google’s AI Overviews to name the best hot rod and custom builders in New Zealand, neither list included you, even though your builds are profiled in editorial media. These tools name a business when the content is about that business: its people, its capability, and how it works. Most coverage of your work, including ours, tells the owner’s story and their build journey, so Kruzin Kustoms appears as a credit rather than the subject.

Opportunity

Put your builds where search and AI tools look

Workshop profiles we publish on NZV8 already appear in AI answers, and 41% of NZ Performance Car’s Google impressions now come from AI-generated results. Your Facebook Page shows a steady run of finished builds, but only six of the 61 project pages on your website were added after 2024. Profiles with Kruzin Kustoms as the subject, on sites these tools already use, put your name in those answers.

Opportunity

An online shop that shows what it’s best at

Your shop carries 1,342 products. The home page features ten of them side by side, including a Harley-Davidson wheel, signwriting paint, a T-shirt, and an LS fitting, which makes it hard for a new visitor to see what the shop specialises in or where to start. Your Google Shopping ads lean towards Harley-Davidson parts. Clear featured ranges, built around your builds and your best sellers, would help shoppers and search engines alike.

Fix needed

Your Google profile, listings, and home page speed

Your Google profile has 90 reviews at an average of 4.4, but it appears not to be claimed, so you can’t update its photos, services, or replies. Your Instagram bio and Yellow listing still show the Kelvin Grove Road address. Your home page loads about 18 MB, most of it the hero video, and Google’s data from real mobile visitors shows it loading more slowly than Google recommends. All of these are quick to fix.

Gap

Tracking for build enquiries

GA4 and Google Ads track shop sales through WooCommerce. There’s no Meta pixel on the site, so Facebook and Instagram can’t show ads to people who’ve visited or viewed a product. We couldn’t see whether enquiry forms or phone calls are tracked, and your public Google ads are mostly shop products plus ads on your own name. The account review will show what they return.

The plan

Foundations first, then content and paid media

The plan covers the three things you’ve asked about: more visibility for your hot rod and custom builds, a move into JDM and performance builds, and a better-performing online shop.

1

Review your accounts and set a 90-day plan

  • A read-only review of Google Ads, GA4, Search Console, and Merchant Center.
  • A review of your online shop: how products are grouped and featured, your Google Shopping feed, and what sells.
  • Written findings and a 90-day plan for your new team member, matched to their skills.
  • A list of website and shop fixes, split into what your team can do in-house and what needs your web developer, each written so it can be quoted.
  • A handover session with you and your team member.
2

Set up tracking and fix your listings

  • Two Meta pixels. Your own connects through WooCommerce and covers the shop, including your product catalogue, so your team can use it for its own advertising. Via Media’s covers the campaigns we run to our audiences.
  • The audiences we build through our campaigns sit in our ad account, and we reuse them for your future campaigns.
  • Tracking for enquiry forms and phone calls in GA4 and Google Ads, tested before handover.
  • A line for your privacy policy covering Meta tracking.
  • Your Google Business Profile claimed and updated, and your address corrected on Instagram and Yellow.
3
Two profiles a quarter, three in the first

Behind-the-Build profiles on NZV8 and NZ Performance Car

  • Kruzin Kustoms is the subject: your team, your process, and the work.
  • NZV8 covers your hot rod and custom builds. NZ Performance Car introduces your JDM and performance work to its audience of about 115,000, most of them aged 25 to 44.
  • We schedule the profiles into our monthly, two-monthly, and quarterly editorial cycles, so each one gets a prime slot across our websites, social channels, and print when it runs. In the first three months we publish three: two on NZV8 and one on NZ Performance Car. From month four, we publish one on each title every quarter.
  • Each profile is a Q&A with you, written as an online article built to be found by search engines and AI tools. It also runs in the print magazine.
  • We work from photos and video your team supplies of the build, your workshop, and your staff, or from our own shoots of builds we feature editorially. You’re welcome to use the photos and video on your own channels.
  • We promote each online article to the matching audience on Facebook and Instagram, with links to your site.
4

Run your build videos to both audiences

  • We cut the build video you already have into Facebook and Instagram ads.
  • The ads run to NZV8’s and NZ Performance Car’s audiences and link to the profiles and your site.
  • Once tracking is live, we also show ads to people who have visited your site, so the campaign works toward enquiries.
5
Optional

Shop and search campaigns

  • Google Shopping campaigns built around the ranges the review shows sell best.
  • Facebook and Instagram product ads that show people the parts they viewed in your shop.
  • Search campaigns for people looking for a builder.
  • We set the price once the review shows your current spend and setup.
6

Monthly reporting

  • One written report each month covering the profiles, the paid social, enquiries, and shop sales.
