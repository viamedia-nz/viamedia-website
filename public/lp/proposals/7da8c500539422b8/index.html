Introducing UltraTube RS to Australian fabricators

A three-month Meta campaign that tells the UltraTube RS story to motorsport and performance car enthusiasts in South East Queensland and Northern New South Wales, and sends them to your online shop.

Who we are

A trusted voice to fabricators.

Via Media publishes NZ Performance Car and NZV8. We have been writing for performance car enthusiasts for up to 30 years, and they follow us for the builds, the parts, and the people behind them.

Our following reaches well beyond New Zealand. That gives us a direct line to Australian fabricators, and the credibility to introduce a new product to them.

51,000+
Australian followers of NZ Performance Car and NZV8.
The opportunity

A new product for a new audience.

Your Google Search and Performance Max campaigns reach buyers who are already searching. Most Australian enthusiasts have not heard of UltraTube RS yet, so they are not searching for it.

Meta puts the product in front of those fabricators first, in the feeds where they already follow car content. Once they know the name, your Google campaigns are there when they search for it. This plan assumes your Google Search and Performance Max campaigns keep running alongside Meta, but that isn't essential. If you would rather move some of that spend across to Meta, we can plan around that.

The plan

From first look to the shop.

Three stages, running together over three months. Each stage feeds the next.

1
Awareness

Put UltraTube RS in front of Australian fabricators

We run partnership ads that show NZ Performance Car and your new Industrial Tube Australia page together. The ads go to our Australian followers in South East Queensland and Northern New South Wales, and we extend the reach with lookalike audiences and interest targeting for motorsport, drifting, drag racing, and fabrication.

2
Education

Explain why it matters

A short video cut from stock or archive motorsport footage with text overlays, and a carousel that sets out the case: a dual-phase steel tube made for safety cages, benchmarked against 4130 chromoly and T45, and predictable to bend and weld.

The fabricators from our New Zealand feature give it credibility. Nick Mitchell of Mitchell Race Xtreme, and Dave McCahon, whose Racecraft Engineering has supplied more than 9,700 roll cage kits, speak from hands-on experience.

3
Conversion

Bring interested fabricators to the shop

People who watch half the video or engage with the posts see product ads with sizes and prices. These link straight to the UltraTube RS page in your online shop.

Where the traffic goes

We recommend starting with your existing shop page, so buyers land one click from the cart. If the first month shows strong interest but few carts, we can add a dedicated explainer page.

Recommended start

UltraTube RS shop page with a short copy block

We supply a short block of copy for the top of your UltraTube RS shop page: the key strength figures, one fabricator quote, and why it suits cage work. It sits above the product table and keeps the path to purchase short.

Option

Dedicated UltraTube RS explainer page

A fuller page on your shop site with fabricator endorsements and the material comparison, linking to the products. We would scope and price this separately if the month-one results point to it.

How we'll measure it

Tracking the whole path to purchase.

UltraTube RS is new to this audience, so we measure each step from first view to sale. You receive a written report each month.

Attention

Reach in the target regions, and cost per person who watches half the video.

Interest

Visits to the UltraTube RS shop page, tracked by the Meta pixel.

Intent

Add to carts and enquiries from the shop page.

Sales

UltraTube RS orders from the target regions, compared with the three months before the campaign.
