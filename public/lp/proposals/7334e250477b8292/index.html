Who we are

Home of New Zealand's modified car community

Via Media publishes NZ Performance Car, NZV8, and Auto Channel. NZ Performance Car is the title New Zealand's performance and modified car community reads, watches, and follows, online, on social, and in print.

We own both the audience and the channels we publish on. That lets us create content about your cars that readers trust, and put paid media in front of the people most likely to act on it.

The opportunity

Reaching owners of older VWs

The Golf has a strong following in New Zealand's enthusiast scene, from early GTIs to the current R, and it turns out in numbers at local events.

Many of those owners drive cars eight years or older, beyond the new-car warranty and service plans. Essential Service is built for exactly these cars, and NZ Performance Car is where their owners already spend time.

The new Golf R gives us a reason to talk to the whole Golf community. Essential Service gives owners of older cars a reason to return to a Volkswagen service centre.

The plan

The Golf R drive, then three months of Essential Service

Part one

Golf R content package

  1. Test drive and shootA full day on location with the Golf R, shooting photography and video.
  2. Web reviewA full review on nzperformancecar.co.nz, written to be found in search and AI-assisted answers.
  3. Short-form videoPublished on NZ Performance Car's Instagram, YouTube, and Facebook.
  4. Paid reelA cut of the video run as a paid ad to the NZ Performance Car audience.
  5. Print reviewFive pages in the Christmas issue of NZ Performance Car (issue 324), on sale in early December.
  6. ReportReach and engagement across the web, social, and paid placements.

Volkswagen New Zealand can use the photos and video on its own channels for organic posts.

Part two

Essential Service campaign, three months

  1. Month one: articleAn article on NZ Performance Car about servicing older Volkswagens with genuine parts and trained technicians. It's written to answer the questions owners search for and ask AI tools.
  2. Every month: paid socialStatic and video ads to the NZ Performance Car audience, linking to the Essential Service page.
  3. Every month: retargeting audiencesWe build audiences in our ad account from people who watch the video ads, so later ads can reach them again.
  4. Every month: on-site placementsA pop-up and 10,000 banner impressions a month on nzperformancecar.co.nz.
  5. Every month: reportA short written report of the results.

Volkswagen supplies the images and video for the Essential Service ads.
