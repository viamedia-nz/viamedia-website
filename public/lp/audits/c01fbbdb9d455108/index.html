Overview

Key points

Ag Steel spent NZ$23,381 on Google ads in the last 12 months. Google is not counting meaningful results, such as enquiries and orders. That needs to be fixed first; then it will be possible to judge how effective the spend is.

Google counts page visits as conversions

99% of the 2,656 conversions Google recorded were visits to the contact page. Your AgSteel Performance Tracker records 25 form enquiries from March to October.

The campaigns optimise to that count

Both Performance Max campaigns are set to find more conversions, so they find more contact-page visits. 55% of their spend ran outside Google Search, on other websites, apps and YouTube.

Most of the visible spend reaches people searching for a named business

54% went on searches for named wineries, orchards and horticulture businesses, and 28% on other steel suppliers. 2% went on searches for posts, trellis, wire or beams.

Organic search and your email list can drive enquiries to the product pages

Organic search already sends most of its visitors to product pages. About 700 Mailchimp contacts carry Ag Steel tags. However, the last full newsletter went out in March 2026; since then, two emails have gone to small vineyard groups, the latest in August.

Your AgSteel Performance Tracker judges results on enquiries and orders, but is not yet capturing true conversion data

See the tracker section below.

Findings

Audit findings

Six findings, listed in order of urgency. Each shows the finding, its impact, and the fixes that address it in the prioritised fix list below.

Select a finding to open it, or open them all.
High1. Google counts page visits as conversions2,642 of 2,656 conversions were contact-page visits
Finding
Google Analytics records a "contact_us" event whenever someone opens a page whose address starts with /contact. Google Ads imports that event as its main conversion. It accounts for 2,642 of 2,656 conversions in 12 months. Contact form submissions have also been counted since 18 May 2026 (34). The order form and newsletter sign-up are not counted.
Impact
Almost every cost-per-lead figure, in Google and in your AgSteel Performance Tracker, is a cost per page visit. A visitor from overseas who opens the contact page counts the same as a grower asking for a quote.
Fixes
1, 2 and 3
High2. The campaigns optimise to page visitsBidding is set to find more of the conversions in finding 1
Finding
Both Performance Max campaigns (Vineyard and Kiwifruit) are set to get as many conversions as possible within a target cost. Together they spent NZ$17,358: 44% on Google Search, 41% on ads on other websites and apps, and 14% on YouTube. The two Search campaigns are set to maximise conversion value, but no value is recorded.
Impact
Google is doing what it is told, with the result that it finds cheap contact-page visits, much of it through ads on other websites and on YouTube. Performance Max clicks rose from about 2,000 to 11,000 a month from August to October, while form enquiries stayed at 2 to 3 a month. Since 18 May, the two campaigns have recorded 8 contact-form submissions.
Fixes
4, after 1 to 3
High3. Most visible spend reaches people searching for a named business2% of visible search spend reached searches for your products
Finding
Of the searches Google reports (NZ$7,617, about a third of spend), most were for a named business rather than one of your products.
Impact
A search for "Craggy Range" is more likely to come from a wine buyer or a job seeker than a grower buying posts. The Vineyard campaign's search themes include "nz wineries", which produces this outcome.
Fixes
5 and 6
Medium4. Coverage of your own products is minimalNo apple or orchard keywords; accessory keywords paused
Finding
Kiwifruit and vineyards are covered; apples and other orchards are not. Keywords for clips, tensioners, caps and braces are paused, though your August email offered free clip installation and "vineyard clips" is searched often. One campaign bids on "industrial tube hamilton", the name of Industrial Tube NZ, a related TGF company.
Impact
Growers searching for products you sell, by crop or by part, are not reached by the ads.
Fixes

Medium5. agsteel.co.nz also loads Industrial Tube NZ's trackingAg Steel visitors are added to a related company's audiences
Finding
Ag Steel's own tracking is set up correctly. agsteel.co.nz also loads the analytics and ad tags of Industrial Tube NZ, a related TGF company.
Impact
Ag Steel visitors are added to Industrial Tube NZ's advertising audiences and reports, which mixes the two brands' data.
Fixes
8
Low6. Organic search and email are underusedProduct pages rank on page 2; last full newsletter sent in March
Finding
Organic search brings about 130 clicks a month, mostly to product pages (Ag Beam, vineyard posts, stringing poles). "Steel vineyard posts" ranks around position 15, and "vineyard clips" shows 332 times with no clicks. In Mailchimp, Ag Steel contacts share one list with Industrial Tube and are grouped by tags. About 700 contacts carry tags that appear to relate to Ag Steel, but the tags do not clearly identify which contacts belong to Ag Steel. The last full newsletter went to 511 contacts in March 2026. Since then, two emails have gone to small vineyard groups: 40 contacts in April and 51 in August. Website sign-up forms have added three contacts to the list in total.
Impact
Two channels you already own, at no media cost, send fewer growers to the product pages than they could.

Notes

Basis of this audit

This audit is based on read-only access to Google Ads, Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager, Search Console and Mailchimp, your AgSteel Performance Tracker, and information you have supplied, for the 12 months to 11 November 2026. It reflects what those sources show. It does not include decisions, context or data held outside them, so a finding may not match how a campaign was intended to run. Where a finding conflicts with what you know, tell us and we will correct it.
Plain-English glossary
Conversion
Whatever Google is told to count as a success. Here, mostly a contact-page visit.
Performance Max
A Google campaign that places ads across Search, YouTube, Maps and partner sites, and chooses where itself.
Ads on other websites and apps
Google's Display Network: banner and image ads shown on sites and apps that carry Google ads.
Search theme
A hint given to Performance Max about what people search for.
Broad keyword
A keyword Google may match to loosely related searches.
Impression share
How often an ad showed, out of the times it could have.
Organic search
The ordinary Google results below the ads, at no media cost.
