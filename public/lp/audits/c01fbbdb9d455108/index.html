Overview

Key points

Ag Steel spent $23,381 on Google ads in the last 12 months. Google is not counting meaningful results, such as enquiries and orders. That needs to be fixed first; then it will be possible to judge how effective the spend is.

Google counts page visits as conversions

99% of the 2,656 conversions Google recorded were visits to the contact page. Your AgSteel Performance Tracker records 25 form enquiries from March to October.

The campaigns optimise to that count

Both Performance Max campaigns are set to find more conversions, so they find more contact-page visits. 55% of their spend ran outside Google Search, on other websites, apps and YouTube. From August to October, their clicks rose from about 2,000 to 11,000 a month, and your recorded enquiries did not rise.

Most enquiries arrive by phone, email or in person, and their source isn’t recorded

Your records show about 30 enquiries a month. From March to October, Google Analytics recorded 25 website form enquiries, about 1 in 11 of the 281 in your records. How each enquiry found you isn’t recorded, so the number that came from the ads can’t be measured.

Most of the visible spend reaches people searching for a named business

54% went on searches for named wineries, orchards and horticulture businesses, and 28% on other steel suppliers. 2% went on searches for posts, trellis, wire or beams.

Organic search and your email list can drive enquiries to the product pages

Organic search already sends most of its visitors to product pages. About 700 Mailchimp contacts carry Ag Steel tags. However, the last full newsletter went out in March 2026; since then, two emails have gone to small vineyard groups, the latest in August.

Your AgSteel Performance Tracker judges results on enquiries and orders, but is not yet capturing true conversion data

See the tracker section below.

VerdictGoogle counts contact-page visits rather than enquiries, and most of the spend goes on clicks unlikely to produce one. When Performance Max clicks rose more than five-fold, your recorded enquiries did not rise. Reduce spend while the tracking is fixed, starting with the Vineyard Performance Max campaign, and move the rest to product and crop searches, so the ads can be judged on real enquiries before the March to July peak.

Findings

Audit findings

Six findings, listed in order of urgency. Each shows the finding, its impact, and the fixes that address it in the prioritised fix list below.

Select a finding to open it, or open them all.
High1. Google counts page visits as conversions2,642 of 2,656 conversions were contact-page visits
Finding
Google Analytics records a ‘contact_us’ event whenever someone opens a page whose address starts with /contact. Google Ads imports that event as its main conversion. It accounts for 2,642 of 2,656 conversions in 12 months. Contact form submissions have also been counted since 18 May 2026 (34). The order form and newsletter sign-up are not counted.
Impact
Almost every cost-per-lead figure, in Google and in your AgSteel Performance Tracker, is a cost per page visit. A visitor from overseas who opens the contact page counts the same as a grower asking for a quote.
Fixes
1, 2 and 3
High2. The campaigns optimise to page visitsBidding is set to find more of the conversions in finding 1
Finding
Both Performance Max campaigns (Vineyard and Kiwifruit) are set to get as many conversions as possible within a target cost. Together they spent $17,358: 44% on Google Search, 41% on ads on other websites and apps, and 14% on YouTube. The two Search campaigns are set to maximise conversion value, but no value is recorded.
Impact
Google is doing what it is told, with the result that it finds cheap contact-page visits, much of it through ads on other websites and on YouTube. Performance Max clicks rose from about 2,000 to 11,000 a month from August to October, while form enquiries stayed at 2 to 3 a month and your recorded enquiries, by all routes, were 26, 26 and 21. Since 18 May, the two campaigns have recorded 8 contact-form submissions: 7 from the Kiwifruit campaign and 1 from the Vineyard campaign, which takes most of the spend.
Fixes
13 now; 4 once 1 to 3 are done

Low6. Organic search and email are underusedProduct pages rank on page 2; last full newsletter sent in March
Finding
Organic search brings about 130 clicks a month, mostly to product pages (Ag Beam, vineyard posts, stringing poles). ‘Steel vineyard posts’ ranks around position 15, and ‘vineyard clips’ shows 332 times with no clicks. Strategy

Making Google ads, organic search and email work

Based on your enquiry and cash-sale figures, the findings above, and how growers buy. The sales figures supplied cover cash sales only; account-client sales were not available, so no order value or return on ad spend is calculated.

Where Google fitsAg Steel reaches growers and contractors through field sales, print, radio and field days; print, radio and events made up 63% of marketing spend from March to October. Google’s job is to reach growers who search for a product or a crop while planning a build, and bring them to the product pages to enquire.
Spend and enquiriesGoogle ads have cost about $2,100 a month since May. Your records show about 30 enquiries a month, most from March to July; you note that the vineyard sector is new and its sales information is limited, so the figures are mostly kiwifruit. From August to October, Performance Max clicks rose more than five-fold and your recorded enquiries did not rise. How each enquiry found you isn’t recorded, so the cost of an enquiry from the ads can’t be determined. Your cash sales, which you expect are mainly new customers, are the closest measure of new business today.
Recommended budgetReduce spend while the tracking is being fixed. Pause the Vineyard Performance Max campaign: it took 58% of Google ad spend in the last 12 months, and 1 of the 8 contact-form submissions recorded from Performance Max since 18 May. Keep the Kiwifruit campaign and the Search campaigns, and move their spend from business names to product and crop searches, adding Search ads for vineyard products. This cuts the spend least likely to produce enquiries without stopping the ads. Set the level for the March to July peak from the first months of corrected data, judged on enquiries by source.
Role of each channelGoogle Search reaches growers searching by product and crop. Organic search already brings about 130 clicks a month to the product pages; improving the vineyard clips, Ag Wire and steel vineyard post pages (fix 9) adds to that at no media cost. Email keeps growers and contractors in contact between seasons, ahead of each build period. Print, radio and field days remain the main way to reach growers; recording how each enquiry found you (fix 12) will show what each channel produces.
Required for executionEdit access to the Ag-Steel Google Ads account, Google Analytics property and Tag Manager container (read-only today), and a record of how each enquiry found you (fix 12). The existing product pages are enough to start; the test enquiry (fix 3) is done together in Stage 2.
Success measuresEnquiries a month by source; form, order-form and call enquiries each counted once in Google Ads; the share of search spend on product and crop searches; cash sales a month through the March to July peak. Three to five targets in the AgSteel Performance Tracker, set after one month of corrected data.
Notes

Basis of this audit

This audit is based on read-only access to Google Ads, Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager, Search Console and Mailchimp, a review of the tags on agsteel.co.nz, your AgSteel Performance Tracker, and information you have supplied, including your sales records (monthly enquiries, and cash-sale orders and values, for November 2025 to October 2026; account-client sales were not available), for the 12 months to 11 November 2026. It reflects what those sources show. It does not include decisions, context or data held outside them, so a finding may not match how a campaign was intended to run. Where a finding conflicts with what you know, tell us and we will correct it.
Plain-English glossary
Conversion
Whatever Google is told to count as a success. Here, mostly a contact-page visit.
Performance Max
A Google campaign that places ads across Search, YouTube, Maps and partner sites, and chooses where itself.
Ads on other websites and apps
Google’s Display Network: banner and image ads shown on sites and apps that carry Google ads.
Search theme
A hint given to Performance Max about what people search for.
Broad keyword
A keyword Google may match to loosely related searches.
Impression share
How often an ad showed, out of the times it could have.
Organic search
The ordinary Google results below the ads, at no media cost.
