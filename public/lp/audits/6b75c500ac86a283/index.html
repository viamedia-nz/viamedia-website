Overview

Key points

Industrial Tube Australia spent $20,901 on Google ads in the last 12 months. Since March 2026, enquiries from the Australian website have not been recorded in the Industrial Tube Australia ad account. That needs to be fixed first; then it will be possible to judge how effective the spend is.

The Australian website carries Industrial Tube NZ's Google Ads tag, not its own

Activity and visitor audiences from the Australian website are recorded in Industrial Tube NZ's ad account instead. Since 18 March, almost all conversions the Australian account records come from people tapping the call button shown in its ads, which Google tracks without the website.

The campaigns are optimising to conversions that aren't being captured

All three live campaigns are set to get as many conversions as possible. While your Industrial Tube Australia Performance Tracker recorded 54 form enquiries from July to October, the ad account recorded 22 conversions, mostly calls from ads. The campaigns have no record of which clicks led to the form enquiries, so they cannot bid towards them.

A large share of visible search spend reaches people searching for other businesses

41% went on searches for other businesses by name, and 33% on relevant product searches. No keywords cover your target industries.

Organic search and email are not being measured or used

The Search Console property only covers the old address, www.industrialtube.com.au. Since the site moved to industrialtube.com.au in March, it has recorded about a quarter of the previous organic clicks. Only one EDM has been sent to Australian contacts in the last 12 months.

Your Industrial Tube Australia Performance Tracker judges results on enquiries and orders, but its Google figures are incomplete

It misses one Search campaign, shows NZ$ figures under A$ labels, and reports Performance Max as Shopping. See the tracker section below.

Findings

Audit findings

Six findings, listed in order of urgency. Each shows the finding, its impact, and the fixes that address it in the prioritised fix list below.

Select a finding to open it, or open them all.
High1. Enquiries from the Australian website are not recorded in the Australian ad accountThe Australian ad account's own tag is missing from the website
Finding
The Australian ad account's tag is not on industrialtube.com.au. Instead, the site loads Industrial Tube NZ's Google Analytics tag, which also passes visits to Industrial Tube NZ's ad account. It is added by site code or a WordPress plugin, not Tag Manager. The account's main conversion, a visit to the contact page, was last triggered on 18 March 2026, around the time the site moved from www.industrialtube.com.au to industrialtube.com.au. Since then, almost all recorded conversions are calls from the call button in the ads (25 in 12 months), which Google tracks without the website. The contact form is not tracked, and the ‘Website calls’ conversion has never recorded a call.
Impact
Activity and visitor audiences from the Australian website are recorded in Industrial Tube NZ's ad account, not the Australian ad account that pays for the ads. Before March, conversions were contact-page visits; since March, the Australian account records almost none from the website. Neither measures enquiries.
Fixes
1, 2 and 3
High2. The campaigns are optimising to conversions that aren't being capturedThree live campaigns are set to maximise conversions the account no longer receives
Finding
The account was relaunched in July with three campaigns (two Search, one Performance Max), all set to get as many conversions as possible. From July to October they spent $8,230 and recorded 22 conversions. Performance Max has no product feed (see finding 5), and 36% of its spend ran on other websites, apps and YouTube. Google's automatic recommendations are switched on, and applied keyword and campaign changes in October without manual approval.
Impact
With almost no conversions to learn from, the bidding has no reliable signal about which clicks lead to enquiries. Changes are also applied automatically, without manual approval.
Fixes
5, after 1 to 3
Medium3. A large share of visible search spend reaches people searching for other businesses41% on other businesses by name; 33% on relevant product searches
Finding
Google's search terms report lists the searches that showed the ads and were clicked, with their cost. The 496 searches with the most clicks cost $4,535. 41% of that (about $1,850) went on searches for other businesses by name, such as a competitor's name. Some of this is deliberate: the account bids on Orrcon Steel, Atlas Steels, Midway Metals and Stirlings Performance Steels. Most keywords are broad match, which extends that to steel businesses in general. 33% went on relevant product searches, such as stainless and steel tube.
Impact
These clicks to the website are recorded; what is not recorded is whether a click led to an enquiry. Spend on people looking for another business rarely produces one, and it reduces the budget available for product searches.
Fixes
6
Medium4. Your target industries and regions are not coveredNo keywords for furniture, medical equipment, ute trays, agricultural machinery or security gates
Finding
There are no live keywords targeting any of your target industries. Motorsport is covered only by ‘roll cage tube’ ($2.68 in 12 months). Locations cover 27 Queensland areas and six in northern New South Wales, with no Newcastle. The location setting also shows ads to people outside these areas who show interest in them.
Impact
Manufacturers in your target industries who search by application are not reached, and some spend reaches people outside your delivery and collection area.
Fixes
7
Medium5. The online shop is not measured, and Shopping ads are not set up for AustraliaNo Google tags on the shop; no Australian product feed
Finding
The online shop at shop.industrialtube.com.au has no Google tags, so purchases, add-to-carts and revenue are not recorded in Google. The only Merchant Centre account holds Industrial Tube NZ's products, with New Zealand shipping only, so there is no Australian product feed. The paused Shopping campaign used that New Zealand feed.
Impact
The ecommerce and Shopping rows in your tracker cannot be filled, and Google cannot show Australian products in Shopping results.
Fixes
8
Low6. Organic search and email are not measured or usedSearch Console only tracks the old www address; one EDM in 12 months
Finding
Search Console is set up only for the old address, www.industrialtube.com.au. Since the site moved to industrialtube.com.au in March, Google shows the new addresses in search results, and clicks on them are not recorded by that property. It now records about 38 organic clicks a month, against about 140 before. Most recorded clicks are for ‘industrial tube’ by name; ‘stainless steel tube’ and ‘steel tube’ show about 800 times each with few clicks. Only one EDM has been sent to Australian contacts in the last 12 months (a December news email to 103 recipients). The ‘ITMAU Online Store’ tag has no contacts, and the Australian site has no sign-up form.
Impact
Organic performance since March cannot be judged, and two channels that cost nothing in media are not bringing buyers back to the site.

Notes

Basis of this audit

This audit is based on read-only access to Google Ads, Google Tag Manager, Search Console, Merchant Centre and Mailchimp, a review of the tags on industrialtube.com.au, your Industrial Tube Australia Performance Tracker, and information you have supplied, for the 12 months to November 2026. The Google Analytics property for the Australian website was not available to us. The audit reflects what these sources show. It does not include decisions, context or data held outside them, so a finding may not match how a campaign was intended to run. Where a finding conflicts with what you know, tell us and we will correct it.
Plain-English glossary
Conversion
Whatever Google is told to count as a success. Before March, mostly a contact-page visit; since March, mostly a call from an ad.
Tag
A small piece of code on the website that tells an ad account when a visitor does something.
Performance Max
A Google campaign that places ads across Search, YouTube, Maps and partner sites, and chooses where itself.
Ads on other websites and apps
Google's Display Network: banner and image ads shown on sites and apps that carry Google ads.
Product feed
A list of products, prices and stock sent to Google Merchant Centre so they can appear in Shopping results.
Broad keyword
A keyword Google may match to loosely related searches.
Search Console property
The version of the website address that Search Console reports on. A property for www.industrialtube.com.au does not cover industrialtube.com.au.
Organic search
The ordinary Google results below the ads, at no media cost.
