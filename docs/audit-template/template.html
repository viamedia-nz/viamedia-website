Overview

Key points

Norvik spent $38,600 on Google and Meta ads in the last 12 months. The ads produce enquiries, but the platforms count the wrong things, and a third of Google spend reaches people unlikely to buy.

Google counts page visits and repeat form submissions as enquiries

Google recorded 3,140 conversions. Your CRM shows 260 enquiries from the website and phone in the same period, about 12 recorded conversions for every real enquiry.

A third of Google spend went on clicks unlikely to produce an enquiry

At least $10,400 of $31,400 went on searches for other businesses and Performance Max ads outside Google Search.

Meta lead ads produce leads that are not followed up

Meta collected 52 leads. Fourteen were contacted within two days, and none reach the CRM automatically.

Two of three product lines are not advertised

Bearings and fasteners have no campaigns, although they receive a quarter of organic search clicks.

The website and email list can do more

Product pages are slow on mobile and a third are not in Google’s index. 2,850 trade contacts receive a newsletter but no reorder reminders.

VerdictGoogle can pay its way at current spend once it counts real enquiries. Move a third of it to product searches, and connect Meta leads to the CRM.

Findings

Audit findings

Six findings, listed in order of urgency. Each shows the finding, its impact, and the fixes that address it in the prioritised fix list below.

Select a finding to open it, or open them all.
High1. Google counts page visits and repeat form submissions as enquiriesAbout 12 recorded conversions for every real enquiry
Finding
Google Ads uses three main conversions: a visit to the contact page, a visit to the quote page, and the quote form. The quote form’s thank-you page reloads when a visitor returns to it, so one enquiry can be counted several times. Calls from the ads are counted correctly. Google recorded 3,140 conversions in 12 months; your CRM shows 260 enquiries from the website and phone.
Impact
Every cost-per-lead figure in Google and in your monthly report is far lower than the real cost. The campaigns are set to find more conversions, so they find more page visits.
Fixes
1 and 3
High2. A third of Google spend went on clicks unlikely to produce an enquirySearches for other businesses, and Performance Max ads outside Search
Finding
Of the searches Google shows, 38% of spend went on searches for other businesses by name or for products Norvik does not sell, such as ‘hydraulic repairs near me’. Most keywords are broad match. Performance Max spent 33% of its budget on ads on other websites, apps and YouTube.
Impact
At least $10,400 of $31,400, and up to an estimated $15,700, went on clicks unlikely to produce an enquiry.
Fixes
4 and 5
High3. Meta leads are not followed up52 leads; 14 contacted within two days; no link to the CRM
Finding
Meta lead ads collected 52 leads in 12 months. The leads stay in Meta until someone downloads them, which happened about once a fortnight. Fourteen leads were contacted within two days and nine became quotes. The Meta pixel is on the website but records page views only.
Impact
Leads go cold before anyone calls. Meta also cannot learn which leads became customers, so it keeps finding people who fill in forms rather than people who buy.
Fixes
2, 3 and 10
Medium4. Two of three product lines are not advertisedNo campaigns for bearings or fasteners
Finding
All campaigns promote hydraulics. Bearings and fasteners have no keywords or ads, although they receive a quarter of organic search clicks. There are no campaigns for your target industries (transport fleets, agriculture, food processing).
Impact
Buyers searching for two thirds of your range, or by industry, are not reached by the ads.
Fixes
6
Medium5. Product pages are slow on mobile, and a third are not indexed6.8 seconds to load; 252 of 664 product pages not in Google’s index
Finding
Product pages take 6.8 seconds to load on mobile, mainly because of large product images. Search Console shows 252 product pages not indexed, mostly filter pages that duplicate the main category pages. The online catalogue has no product feed in Merchant Centre.
Impact
Slow pages lose visitors from both ads and organic search, and products not in the index or in Merchant Centre cannot be found.
Fixes
7 and 8
Low6. The email list is used for news, not reorders2,850 trade contacts; no automated emails
Finding
The list holds 2,850 trade contacts, tagged by branch but not by industry or product line. Eleven newsletters went out in 12 months, with a 3.8% average click rate. There are no reorder reminders, quote follow-ups or welcome emails.
Impact
Repeat trade buyers are the easiest sales to win, and the list is not prompting them.

Strategy

Making Google, Meta, organic search and email work

Based on your enquiry and order data, and the findings above.

Recommended budgetKeep Google at about $2,600 a month, with a third moved from Performance Max into Search campaigns by product line. Keep Meta at about $600 a month, moved from lead forms to past website visitors and people similar to existing customers.
Role of each channelGoogle Search captures buyers looking for a product. Meta keeps Norvik in front of likely buyers between purchases. Email prompts reorders. Organic search supports all three through faster, indexed product pages.
Required for executionA landing page for each product line with a short quote form; lead source recorded in the CRM; product images resized for mobile.
Success measuresCost per qualified enquiry under $85; 70% of leads contacted within two hours; organic clicks to product pages up 25% in six months; reorder emails producing 10 orders a month.

About this sample

This sample shows the format and depth of a Via Media digital platform audit. Norvik Trade & Industrial Supply, its people and its figures are fictional, based on patterns we see in real audits. A real audit is based on read-only access to your ad accounts, analytics, Search Console and email platform, your own reports, and information you supply.
Plain-English glossary
Conversion
Whatever an ad platform is told to count as a success.
Performance Max
A Google campaign that places ads across Search, YouTube, Maps and partner sites, and chooses where itself.
Ads on other websites and apps
Google’s Display Network: banner and image ads shown on sites and apps that carry Google ads.
Lead ad
A Meta ad with a form inside Facebook or Instagram, so people can enquire without visiting the website.
Indexed
Stored by Google so the page can appear in search results.
Product feed
A list of products, prices and stock sent to Google Merchant Centre so they can appear in Shopping results.
Broad keyword
A keyword Google may match to loosely related searches.
Organic search
The ordinary Google results below the ads, at no media cost.
